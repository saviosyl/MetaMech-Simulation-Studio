## 1) Simulation Tool Positioning Brief (1-page)

### Product
**MetaMech Studio** — industrial layout and flow simulation for equipment lines and factory systems.

### Who it is for

Primary users:

- Process engineers
- Layout engineers
- System integrators
- Technical pre-sales/demo engineers
- Manufacturing teams validating line concepts before implementation

Economic/approval stakeholders:

- Engineering managers
- Operations managers
- Plant/project decision makers

### Key pain points

1. Layout decisions are often made with limited visual proof.
2. Teams discover connection/flow mistakes too late in the process.
3. Stakeholder communication is slow and fragmented (CAD screenshots, slides, verbal explanation).
4. Rework risk increases when transfer logic is not tested visually before execution.
5. Demoing solutions to customers is inconsistent without a structured simulation narrative.

### Core value proposition

**MetaMech Studio helps engineering teams design, simulate, and present industrial layouts faster and with more confidence before physical deployment.**

### Top customer benefits

1. Faster concept-to-validation cycle for line layout decisions.
2. Clear visual proof of flow behavior and handoff points.
3. Better stakeholder alignment through polished simulation views and exports.
4. Reduced rework risk by validating connectivity and behavior early.
5. Stronger pre-sales/customer demos using repeatable, productized storytelling.

### Simple commercial positioning

**A professional industrial simulation workspace that combines layout building, flow validation, and presentation-ready output in one tool.**

### Headline options (short)

1. Design, Simulate, and Present Industrial Systems Faster.
2. Industrial Simulation Built for Real Engineering Workflows.
3. Validate Factory Flow Before You Build.
4. Turn Layout Concepts into Decision-Ready Simulations.
5. From Line Concept to Presentation in One Workspace.

### Subheadline options (short)

1. Build layouts, test flow behavior, and present polished simulation outcomes with confidence.
2. A practical simulation tool for engineering teams who need faster validation and clearer stakeholder communication.
3. Visualize, verify, and communicate conveyor and machine flow in a single professional environment.
4. Reduce design uncertainty by simulating connection and transfer behavior before implementation.
5. Create presentation-ready factory simulation scenarios without fragmented toolchains.

## 3) Demo Script Pack

## A) 3-Minute Overview Demo

Objective:

- Create immediate confidence and interest in under 3 minutes.

Story flow and order:

1. **Open with finished scenario** (show polished scene first, not blank canvas).
2. **Run simulation** and narrate flow path from infeed to outfeed.
3. **Show one meaningful adjustment** (small parameter change with visible impact).
4. **Show presentation/export angle** (camera movement or output-ready view).
5. **Close with pricing path** (1-Day Trial -> Full Access monthly/yearly).

What not to show:

- Deep settings menus
- Unrelated edge-case tooling
- Debug/experimental views

Closing CTA:

- **Start a 1-Day Trial and evaluate the full workflow today.**

---

## B) 10-Minute Standard Product Demo

Objective:

- Demonstrate practical day-to-day workflow value for engineering users.

Story flow and order:

1. **Context (1 min):** business problem and use case.
2. **Build/edit layout (2 min):** place key modules/equipment.
3. **Connect nodes/ports (2 min):** establish clear process flow path.
4. **Run simulation (2 min):** show flow behavior and explain outcome.
5. **Refine quickly (1.5 min):** adjust one important parameter and re-run.
6. **Present/export (1 min):** show polished output view.
7. **Commercial close (0.5 min):** trial and full access options.

What not to show:

- New/unreleased capabilities
- Long technical troubleshooting
- Overly broad "show everything" tours

Closing CTA:

- **Start your 1-Day Trial, then continue on Full Access monthly or yearly once validated.**

---

## C) 20-Minute Customer-Focused Demo

Objective:

- Map the product directly to a specific customer workflow and buying intent.

Story flow and order:

1. **Customer context framing (2 min):** their line type, constraints, decision goals.
2. **Scenario setup aligned to their use case (4 min).**
3. **Connection and transfer validation walkthrough (4 min).**
4. **Simulation run + interpretation of results (4 min).**
5. **Presentation output for stakeholder communication (3 min).**
6. **Adoption path + commercial close (3 min).**

What not to show:

- Generic features unrelated to customer scope
- Internal implementation details
- Long UI detours that dilute business value

Closing CTA:

- **Run a focused 1-Day Trial against your scenario, then move to Full Access for continuous project execution.**

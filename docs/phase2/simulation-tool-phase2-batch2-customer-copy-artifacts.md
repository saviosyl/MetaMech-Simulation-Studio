## 3) FAQ Final Wording (Simulation Tool)

### What is MetaMech Studio used for?
MetaMech Studio is used to design industrial layouts, simulate product flow, and present decision-ready visuals before physical implementation.

### Who is MetaMech Studio for?
It is designed for process and layout engineers, system integrators, and teams that need faster simulation-based validation and clearer stakeholder communication.

### What is included in the 1-Day Trial?
The 1-Day Trial provides full access so you can evaluate the complete Simulation Tool workflow on real scenarios.

### What happens when the trial ends?
Trial access ends automatically. You can continue with Full Access on monthly or yearly billing.

### What does Full Access include?
Full Access provides ongoing use of MetaMech Studio for layout, simulation, and presentation workflows.

### What is the difference between monthly and yearly?
Both provide Full Access. Monthly offers flexibility, while yearly is recommended for continuous use and planning.

### Can we use customer-specific scenarios or models?
Yes. You can work with customer-specific simulation scenarios and project configurations as part of your workflow.

### Is support or onboarding available?
Yes. Guidance and onboarding support can be provided to help teams start quickly and use the tool effectively.

## 6) Tone and Messaging Rules (Copy Consistency Guide)

## Tone
- Professional
- Practical
- Confident
- Clear and direct

## Style rules
- Use short, concrete sentences.
- Lead with outcomes, then mention capability.
- Keep claims realistic and engineering-grounded.
- Prefer workflow language over marketing hype.

## Words to favor
- Validate
- Simulate
- Flow
- Layout
- Decision-ready
- Practical
- Confidence
- Stakeholder alignment

## Words to avoid
- Revolutionary
- Magic
- Guaranteed ROI
- Best-in-class (without proof)
- Buzzword-heavy language

## How to describe the product simply
**MetaMech Studio is an industrial simulation workspace that helps teams build layouts, validate flow behavior, and present decisions clearly before implementation.**
